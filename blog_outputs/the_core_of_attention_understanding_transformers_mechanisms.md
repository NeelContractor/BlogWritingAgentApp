## Understanding Self-Attention in Transformers  

Self-attention is the mechanism that focuses on specific parts of the input, enabling the model to prioritize relevant information. It uses dot products to map the input to the output, allowing the model to prioritize the most relevant parts. This mechanism is essential for handling long-range dependencies, ensuring that the model can focus on the most important information. Self-attention also helps the model focus on the most important parts of the input.
